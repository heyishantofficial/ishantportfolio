A dream campaign is an idea for a brand that hasn't asked for it. The page adds a note at the top saying so, using the `brand:` you set.

## Why this brand

What do you love about it, and what's it getting wrong (or not doing yet)?

## The insight

The human truth the campaign stands on.

> The insight in one line.

## The idea

The campaign in a sentence. Then the name, the line, and what people would actually see.

## How it rolls out

- **Launch:** the first thing people see
- **Social:** the formats, the creators, the hook
- **On ground or in product:** where it meets real life

Mock-ups and AI frames go here. Put them in `public/posts/dream-campaign-template/` and add them like this: `![what it shows](frame-1.jpg "caption")`.

## Why it would work

What changes for the brand if this runs.
